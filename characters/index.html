The characters of Takeout Fox

A fox with a teal delivery backpack, a grandmother with a noodle shop, thirty raccoons who smelled dinner, and a city full of hungry customers. Here is who is who.

Marm

The fastest courier in Lanternfall and the hero of the game. Marm is a young fox in a yellow hoodie and a red cap, with a teal insulated backpack that keeps the noodles hot. He talks to himself while he runs (“Note to self: buy a scooter”) and takes every crash personally.

Grandma Pim

She runs the noodle shop where the story starts. Her last order of the night is extra-spicy and extra-hot, and she expects it to arrive that way.

The Hangry Crowd

Thirty raccoons woken by the smell of Grandma Pim’s noodles. They chase Marm across every district, shouting things like “Just ONE bite!” and “We have coupons!”. One stumble lets them close in.

The customers

Every 350 metres someone is waiting on a rooftop: a student before an exam, an opera singer, a baker who says his bread is better, a grumpy gardener and Grandma Pim’s regulars. Each delivery is worth five tips.

The pigeons and the seagull

Lanternfall’s birds have opinions. The Pigeon Union holds votes, and a seagull keeps trying to order takeout for himself. You will meet them in the cutaway gags.
