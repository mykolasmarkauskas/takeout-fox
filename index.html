How to play in ten seconds

Marm runs by himself and keeps getting faster. You only steer: change lanes, jump and slide. Grab the gold tips along the way, hand the order over when a customer waves, and don’t hit the same trouble twice in a row: one bump makes Marm stumble, a second one within three seconds and the Hangry Crowd piles on.

A city that changes as you run

Every 700 metres the rooftops hand you over to a new district. The sky shifts, the houses change, and a red gate with the district’s name tells you where you are.

Noodle Roofs

Red tiled roofs, lantern strings and chimneys that puff steam from a hundred kitchens. Watch for chimneys in your lane and stray crates.

Harbour Lights

A purple evening over the water: wooden fishing boats bob below the rooftops, bollards line the edge and fish barrels block the way.

Garden Terraces

Green sky, bonsai trees in terracotta pots and flower carts parked right where you want to run.

Frequently asked questions

Is Takeout Fox free?

Yes. Takeout Fox is completely free to play in your browser. There is no download, no account and no ads.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It runs in the browser on iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, and on Windows, Mac, Linux and Chromebook computers. On a phone you play with swipes.

Is it really endless?

Yes. There is no last level: the city keeps generating rooftops, the three districts take turns, and Marm keeps getting faster up to his top speed.

How is the score counted?

Your score is the distance you ran in metres, multiplied by 1 plus 2% for every tip you collected. 1,000 metres with 50 tips scores 2,000.

What ends a run?

Hitting an obstacle or missing a jump over a gap makes Marm stumble and lets the Hangry Crowd close in. A second hit within three seconds ends the run.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open takeoutfox.com and press Play. The game loads in a few seconds and keeps your best scores on your device.
